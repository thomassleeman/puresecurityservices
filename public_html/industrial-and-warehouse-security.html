Learn More about our Industrial & Warehouse Security Services...

Industrial sites and warehouses contain a variety of different goods and equipment. The nature of these sites means a lot of expensive stock is kept on-site, because of that they can be susceptible to crime. Unlike other sites where opportunists may engage in criminality, warehouses and industrial sites can come under the gaze of organised gangs - expensive equipment and stock that can be stolen and sold-on in the blackmarket.

Pure’s security solutions are able to adapt depending on your site's risk levels, which can vary dramatically throughout the year, for example there may be increased risk during the run up to Christmas or through certain points in the summer. Whether you need an entire security team during your busy periods, or simply a mobile patrol during your quieter periods, we can tailor a package to suit your particular needs.

Security check-list:

  1. SIA licences, CRB checks, right to work checks, English language tests and personality profiles are fundamental to our recruitment process for Security Guards.
  2. All our security guards are covered by liability insurance
  3. A fully invested technology solution: all our guards are backed by a 24/7 control room that checks on their status and can be alerted to any incidents or emergencies.

Companies working in a wide range of sectors have benefited from our services, whether retail, construction or other commercial spaces - this has given us the experience necessary to offer tailored solutions, whatever your needs.
