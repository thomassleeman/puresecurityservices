Living Wage pay for Your Security Operatives

When you receive your quotation from Pure Security, we will always include an option for your Security Operatives to be paid the London Living Wage, with a clear explanation of what your operatives will be paid with and without choosing this option.

The cost of living in London is high, and yet so many of the jobs that the capital relies upon are poorly paid.

With Pure Security you can rest assured that, as well as fair pay, your Security Operatives will be able to access all of the benefits that everyone should be able to take for granted, like paid annual leave, compassionate leave, a proper employment contract and proper training. Unfortunately in the Security industry this is not always the case.
